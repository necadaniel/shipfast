# EnvSync: Sync & Secure Your Environment Files Effortlessly

**EnvSync** is a developer-focused app designed to streamline the way you manage, sync, and protect your `.env` files across multiple devices and teams. Built around security, simplicity, and seamless integration, EnvSync empowers developers and small teams to stop copying secrets via email, cloud notes, or unsafe channels.

---

## Vision

EnvSync solves the frustrating pain of environment file management—allowing you to **sync secrets securely and instantly** between your PC, MacBook, and any other device, with zero risk of accidental exposure.

Developers switch contexts, environments, and locations daily. Your secrets deserve a solution built for the real world: _cross-device, fast, and totally secure_.

---

## Core Features

- **End-to-End Encryption**  
  Client-side AES-256 encryption of all environment variable data. Only you hold the keys.

- **Project-Based Organization**  
  Create, manage, and group .env files by project, workspace, or client for maximum control.

- **Cross-Device Sync**  
  Rapid, real-time synchronization between all registered devices (Windows, macOS, Linux, mobile).

- **Web App & CLI Tool**  
  Full-featured web interface for manual editing, plus a CLI utility for your local workflow.

- **One-Click Device Auth**  
  Easily link new devices via QR or secure code. Remote revocation of lost/breached devices.

- **Versioning & Change History**  
  Track changes to your secrets with audit logs and rollback capability.

- **Collaborative Sharing**  
  Invite trusted collaborators to shared projects using role-based permissions (admin/read/write).

- **Zero-Knowledge Architecture**  
  All secrets are always encrypted before reaching EnvSync servers; we never see or store your unencrypted secrets.
